Warriors of the realm

The live warrior list, just like the BBS: every hero from every cartridge, ranked by experience (then level, then dragon kills). Pick (U)PLOAD HERO in the town square once a day and scan the code here to keep your hero current. Each upload replaces that hero's older one, so experience lost to a death, or reset by slaying the Red Dragon, shows here too. Heroes not uploaded for 3+ days are greyed out. Tap a row for full stats.
